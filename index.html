// 03. MASTER'S THESIS SPOTLIGHT

Robotic Solution for Autonomous EV Charging

M.TECH THESIS FLAGSHIP

Autonomous EV Charging Robotic Manipulator

Robotics Laboratory, IIT Bombay • Guide: Prof. Anirban Guha • May 2025 – Jun 2026

System Architecture & Methodology

Autonomous electric vehicle (EV) charging demands reliable, sub-millimeter plug-to-receptacle alignment in the presence of vehicle positioning uncertainties, suspension deflection, and varying approach angles. This thesis designs, simulates, and experimentally validates a compact, high-precision tendon-driven hybrid robotic manipulator.

Quaternion-Inspired Hybrid Manipulator

Designed a novel hybrid robotic manipulator with dual-motor tendon-driven actuation tailored for autonomous EV charging-plug alignment, achieving sub-millimeter positional accuracy under load.

Dual Kinematic Configuration Comparison

Architected and compared two manipulator configurations (universal-joint and spherical-joint variants) through systematic workspace boundary analysis and dexterity metric evaluation.

Custom 3D Motion-Tracking Optical Test Rig

Built a custom 3D motion-tracking test rig utilizing a multi-camera Qualisys optical motion capture system for sub-millimeter 6-DOF ground truth validation.

Neural Network Kinematic Modeling (PyTorch)

Trained deep neural network models on 10,000+ experimental data points, effectively overcoming cable stretch and non-linear routing friction to achieve validation accuracy within ±1.5 mm RMSE.
